\cvitem{purpose}{
  \textbf{Backward Training Principle --- Domain-Specific Model Factory.}
  Formal inversion of conventional curriculum learning: train exclusively at the
  \emph{pure-intent regime} (scalar objective, full capability envelope, unambiguous
  feedback); every constrained sub-regime inherits $\epsilon$-competence by feasibility
  inclusion, not generalisation. Five proved theorems: Pure-Intent Envelope
  ($\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{D}_c)\subseteq\mathcal{F}(\mathcal{D}^\star)$), Constraint
  Cascade, Backward Training Inclusion (KKT non-expansive projection), Forward Training
  Collapse (positive VC failure-gap lower bound), Sample Complexity ($(N{+}1)\times$
  linear; $2^{N-1}\times$ exponential for binary-nested cascades). Canonical case:
  F1 circuit as pure-intent regime for all autonomous driving sub-tasks; derives from
  the theoretical minimum lap time derivation. Aperture Foundation Model + Domain
  Connector interface + Resolver Registry.
  \href{https://github.com/fullscreen-triangle/purpose}{github.com/fullscreen-triangle/purpose}
}

\cvitem{zangalewa}{
  \textbf{Minimum Sufficient Interceptor (MSI) --- Buhera OS Boundary.}
  The OS presents a blank screen; users type natural language; the MSI extracts
  semantic coordinates in $[0,1]^3$ via hierarchical $k$-ary specialist cascade
  ($O(\log_k N)$ small-model invocations) and delivers typed coordinates to the
  vaHera kernel. Sufficiency Theorem: interceptor capacity
  $O(D{\cdot}b{\cdot}\log|\Sigma|)$, independent of downstream state-space size
  (${\sim}10^7$--$10^8$ parameters suffice for OS-scale substrates). Session
  trajectory via precision-by-difference timing; proved-complete four-state
  focus-arbitration machine. Secondary application: autonomous code-repair pipeline
  (exception $\to$ LLM diagnosis $\to$ patch $\to$ git-isolated apply $\to$ rollback).
  \href{https://github.com/fullscreen-triangle/zangalewa}{github.com/fullscreen-triangle/zangalewa}

\cvitem{verum}{
  \textbf{Autonomous Systems: Backward Trajectory Completion.}
  Eliminates forward prediction by completing trajectories \emph{backward}
  from target states in partition space ($\lambda_\mathrm{partition}=0$,
  no chaos). Vehicle modelled as oscillatory circuit graph; full state
  reconstructed from 33\,\% surface observations (Banach contraction,
  $\lambda\in[0.3,0.7]$). 64/64 validations: membrane semiconductor
  (47.8$\times$ rectification; 127-op ALU; quantum coherence eliminated),
  molecular navigation (exhaust trail path-following, 0.15\,m RMS),
  Formula One Bahrain GP (Verstappen 2023; fault detected 13~laps ahead;
  optimal lap time $2.5\,\%$ gap). F1 circuit is the canonical pure-intent
  regime for all driving: road and urban driving are strict feasibility
  restrictions.
  \href{https://vesicle.vercel.app}{vesicle.vercel.app}
