  \textbf{ML for Cancer Genomics: Neoantigen, Resistance, and Therapy Identification.}
  Spectral DFT on three physicochemical channels; 36-dimensional cosine similarity
  $\rho$; $\mathcal{O}(cL\log L)$.

  \textbf{Resistance:} drug-enzyme spectral complementarity $\rho\geq0.50$;
  validated against TEM-1/Ampicillin, GyrA/Ciprofloxacin, AAC(3)-IV/Gentamicin.
  \textbf{Therapy identification:} disease vector $\mathbf{D}\in[0,1]^8$;
  AUC$=1.00$; sparse $\ell_1$ LP for minimum-intervention therapy selection.
  Live: \href{https://syndrome-seven.vercel.app/tools}{syndrome-seven.vercel.app/tools}.
