  $\epsilon$-competence by \emph{feasibility inclusion}, not generalisation.
  Five proved theorems: Pure-Intent Envelope, Constraint Cascade, Backward

\cvitem{verum}{
  \textbf{State Reconstruction from Partial Sensor Coverage.}
  Vehicle as 20-node oscillatory circuit graph; node potential
  $V_i = -\log_2 P(\sigma_i)$; edge conductances from a universal
  transport formula unifying electrical, thermal, viscous, and diffusive
  coupling. Trajectory completion operator $T = P \circ B \circ K$
  is a Banach contraction ($\lambda\in[0.3,0.7]$): full machine state
  recovered from 33\,\% surface observations alone.
  Validated on 2023 Formula One telemetry (Bahrain GP, 13,794 samples):
  convergence residual $7.2\times 10^{-9}$; ICE-Turbo fault detected
  13~laps before event; suspension--aero load correlation $r=0.9997$.
  Same architecture applies to sealing equipment condition monitoring
  from sparse pressure, temperature, and vibration sensors.
  \href{https://github.com/fullscreen-triangle/verum}{github.com/fullscreen-triangle/verum}
}

\cvitem{gas-giant}{
  \textbf{First-Principles Fluid Dynamics and Gas Theory.}
  Derives kinetic gas theory and fluid dynamics from bounded phase space geometry:
  $T = (\hbar/k_B)\cdot dM/dt$ (temperature as processing rate); $PV = Nk_BT$
  from categorical transitions; viscosity $\mu = \tau_c \times g$ (partition lag
  $\times$ coupling strength, not phenomenological); Kirchhoff circuit laws mapped
  to Navier-Stokes; Stokes-Einstein diffusion and thermal conductivity derived.
  Validation: $U/(\tfrac{3}{2}Nk_BT) = 1.000 \pm 0.2\%$; adiabatic expansion
  0.1--0.3\% error; viscosity 2.9\,\% MAE across 12 pure liquids;
  Poiseuille flow 0.9\% mean error. Zero empirical parameters.
  Directly applicable to seal-face fluid dynamics simulation without CFD fitting.
  Live: \href{https://gas-giant.vercel.app/simulate}{gas-giant.vercel.app/simulate}.
}
