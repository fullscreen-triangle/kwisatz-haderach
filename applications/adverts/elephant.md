Is it morally wrong to be strong ? to have purpose and fullfill it ? 

You suddenly arise from a deep sleep, to find yourself in the midst of some savannah bush. You take off your sunglasses, to realise that, it is in fact, true, that you are in the middle of somewhere, only to have your confusion punctured by the trumpet from a young bull elephant.

The spectacular fact of your condition, is that, the bull has no understanding of the concept of property, implying, an altercation is promised. So you implement the ideas you absorbed in kindergarten, you start turning left, and break into a circular 400m sprint, at a pace so criminal, Wade van Niekerk would never believe you. 

With each complete loop, you slowly increase the diameter of the circle. For the elephant, it is unfortunate that, the discoveries of Newton require any object,of such mass and velocity, to endure hardships when it comes to curves. 

Replace yourself with atoms, and your action becomes the idea of melting/deformation. The further you go beyond your center of activity, the less likely it is for you to return to that point. 

Now imagine, a spectactor, touched by your condition, handed you a brand new Ducatti Desmocedici, one of 20 that have been manufactured, and blessed by four popes and the Grand Mufti of Medina, and personally signed and handed over to you by Valentino Rossi. 

You could speed off and arrive in Johannesburg before dinner, but the burden to entertain the spectactors , and the idea of living in a society with enough exhilerating events, beckons you to eternally drive in a circle, going faster and faster with every loop, till your vision becomes time itself.

What we can promise is, a sturdy set of bearings, that go around, despite any imaginable problem, for it is their purpose. Our bearings should never be a discussion, need no mentioning. So incredible it is problematic, for you never have to worry or replace them, they do the tasks that need no honour, no thanks, and yet, never has so much been owed by so many to so few.
