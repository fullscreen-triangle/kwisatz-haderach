It so happens, that you won a prize, 10 tickets to the ISS with your favourite people and 2 tickets for your dogs, and the problem before you is how you will arrive, on the condition that you have to do so, in style.

Say you are buddies with Dom from Fast and Furious, and since you are family, he hooks you up with a Subaru that can drive up a wall, better than a G-Wagon, or even better, just ignore physics and drive straight up at a generous 80km/h, since this is not a race and there is no one to impress, or curves for one to drift. 

Or, say, you decide to show off and walk all the way up to the ISS ? If we decided to be practical, there is the option to take a space shuttle, a soyuz craft, or spaceX falcon 9, with the choice being left to whether you prefer taking off from Kazakstan or somewhere below the Mason-Dixon Line.

Getting to space, in order of mentions, will take around 1h by car, 16 hours walking and maximum 10 minutes with any of the craft, BUT, for a standard approach to the ISS, regardless of mode of transport, you will need to part with at least  24 hours.

The best use of your time, would be to write something down, items that need not be recorded by devices, items that point to your human condition. Whats the best tool to write in space ? A pencil ? The carbon particles and the open wires are a Tinder match for disaster. A ballpoint pen ? PERFECT. The tool works because the ball, at the point, is the most advanced alloy, manufactured to such strict prerequisites, secondly only to modern semiconductor chips in complexity, and the only product that is required to have PERFECT mechanical efficiency.

Without ink, one could still write with a ballpoint pen, and inspect the grooves made in the paper with a microscope. Now imagine, a scenerio in which, any rotating device, is in the safe company of millions of ballpoints ? 

We have embraced perfection, are you prepared for it ?
