\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
Caching was shown to reduce read latency by 30\%~\cite{smith2020}.
Lee proved that LRU eviction is optimal for stack workloads~\cite{lee2021}.
It is well known that larger caches always help.
In \cref{sec:results} we show that the bound is tight.
The method outperforms every baseline.
